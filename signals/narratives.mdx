**Radar › Narratives** is the desk's map of attention: top down from X (the metas people name) and bottom up from the tape (clusters of tokens moving together). It sits next to [Stock tokens](/signals/stock-token-radar) under **Radar**, the discover section.

## The table

One row per narrative, ranked by attention by default (sort by Δ posts, volume or newest with the chips above the table):

| Column | What it is |
|---|---|
| **narrative** | The name and a one-line description; the chip says **emerging** (one leg) or **confirmed** (both legs) |
| **posts / 24h** | Measured posts naming the meta in the last 24 hours, with the change against the previous day |
| **vol 24h** | 24h volume across the narrative's tokens |
| **14d** | A sparkline of the measurement over two weeks |
| **tokens** | The tokens in it, flagship first; a chip opens the dossier |
| **drivers** | The accounts carrying it |
| **age** | Since the narrative was first seen |
| **legs** | Which legs support it: the X read, the tape, or both |

Click a row for its history and the tree of related metas. A narrative that loses both legs **fades**; "show fading" keeps them in the table, and the week's dead metas sit in a fold below it. The footer line states the scan mode (top-down on or off, X measurement on or off) and when each scan last ran, so a quiet table reads as "not scanned" rather than "nothing happening".

## How to read it

A narrative explains attention; it does not clear a token. A token sitting in a confirmed narrative tells you why it is moving and who is talking, and the dossier still decides whether it is safe. The dossier's Project section links a token to its narrative when it is in one.
