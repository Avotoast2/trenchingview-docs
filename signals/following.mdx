**Following** is the desk's reading of a roster of projects: the CoinGecko top 400 (refreshed monthly, never removed), VC-backed tokens, and onchain projects followed by hand. Every official voice of each project is read — the official X account, the site feed or blog, GitHub releases, Telegram — and every item is classified, deduplicated and either **pushed**, kept for the **digest**, or **ignored** by a rule table. The page shows the result.

## Events

The live feed of what the projects said and what the table decided, newest first. Each row carries:

- **The decision** — `push` (sent to the Telegram channel), `digest` (kept for the daily summary), `ignore` (noise, repeats, patch releases). Hover the decision for the rule that made it.
- **The kind** — launch · listing · partnership · tokenomics · governance · security · team · other. The chips above the feed filter by kind.
- **The source** — which account or feed said it, and whether it is a *declared* source (listed by the project), *claimed* (the account's own bio) or *tagged* (added by the desk).
- **The reaction** — the price at the event and the move since, when the project has a market.

Filters: search (project, ticker, words, `@handle`), decision, source kind, 1 / 7 / 30 days. The **movers** strip above the feed lists the pushed events with the largest move in the last two days. The feed polls every 20 seconds while the tab is visible; the title shows how many events arrived since you last looked.

Pressing `o` on a focused row opens the post, `c` previews the chart.

## Narratives

What attention is on right now — top down from X (the metas people name) and bottom up from the tape (clusters of tokens moving together). Each narrative shows posts per 24h, 24h volume across its tokens, a 14-day sparkline, the tokens in it, the drivers, its age and which legs (X, tape, both) support it. **Emerging** narratives have one leg; **confirmed** have both. A narrative that loses both legs fades; the week's dead metas sit in a fold below the table.

A narrative is an input, never a verdict: a token being in a live narrative explains attention, it does not clear the token.

## Projects

The roster. **Attention** lists the rows with a reason to look: onchain follows, quiet for 30+ days, muted, parked, added in the last 7 days, moved 10 %+ today. The other views are **vc-backed** (CoinGecko portfolio tags; the "VC · n" chip names the funds), **active**, **parked** (onchain projects whose pool died) and **all**. Search covers every project regardless of view.

Click a project for its page: the sources read, the events, and the last 60 items its sources said with how each was read. The ⋯ menu on any row opens the project's site, X, docs, chart, explorer and dossier, copies the contract, and adds the token to your watchlist.

## Where it goes

Pushed events reach the Telegram channel with the `following` tag. The project page links to the token's dossier when it has a contract, and a dossier of a followed project links back here.
