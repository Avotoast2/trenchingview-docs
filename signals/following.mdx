**Following** is the desk's reading of a roster of projects: the CoinGecko top 400 (refreshed monthly, never removed), VC-backed tokens, and onchain projects followed by hand. Every official voice of each project is read — the official X account, the site feed or blog, GitHub releases, Telegram — and every item is classified, deduplicated and either **pushed**, kept for the **digest**, or **ignored** by a rule table. Three tabs show the result: **Events**, **Projects**, **Watchlist**.

## Events

The live feed of what the projects said and what the table decided, newest first. A KPI strip on top: pushed today, digest, live and emerging narratives, projects followed. Each row carries:

- **The decision** — `push` (sent to the Telegram channel), `digest` (kept for the daily summary), `ignore` (noise, repeats, patch releases). Hover the decision for the rule that made it.
- **The kind** — launch · listing · partnership · tokenomics · governance · security · team · other. The chips above the feed filter by kind.
- **The source** — which account or feed said it, and whether it is a *declared* source (listed by the project), *claimed* (the account's own bio) or *tagged* (added by the desk).
- **The reaction** — the price at the event and the move since, when the project has a market.

Filters: search (project, ticker, words, `@handle`), decision, source kind, 1 / 7 / 30 days. The **movers** strip above the feed lists the pushed events with the largest move in the last two days. The feed polls every 20 seconds while the tab is visible; the title shows how many events arrived since you last looked.

Pressing `o` on a focused row opens the post, `c` previews the chart.

## Projects

The roster. **Attention** lists the rows with a reason to look: onchain follows, quiet for 30+ days, muted, parked, added in the last 7 days, moved 10 %+ today. The other views are **vc-backed** (CoinGecko portfolio tags; the "VC · n" chip names the funds), **active**, **parked** (onchain projects whose pool died) and **all**. Search covers every project regardless of view.

Click a project for its page. It opens with a **masthead**: a breadcrumb back to the roster with, on the right, when the desk last read the project and last pushed something from it; then the project's name large, its ticker, CoinGecko rank, chain and status, with what it is, why it is held and where it lives on the line under the name (the labels that also drive peers and narratives). The actions sit on the same row — **Watch**, **Ask** (TrenchBrain with the project pre-filled) and **Run a dossier** (or the latest verdict with its age when one exists) — and everything else is in the **⋯ menu**: copy the link, alerts, the project's site, X, docs and community, its markets and explorer, copy the contract, preview the chart, the dossier page, a watch. Under the masthead the **price** with its 24h change beside it and the 7d / 30d / 1y changes as chips, the source and how old the quote is; then a **strip of numbers** — market cap, FDV, float (the share of fully diluted value in circulation), 24h volume against market cap, distance from the all-time high with its date, liquidity for onchain tokens, TVL and fees when DefiLlama lists the project. The **profile** follows: two sentences on what the project is (the dossier's own description when the token has one, CoinGecko's otherwise; *more* unfolds the full text) on the left, and on the right a labelled list — **categories**, **backed by** (the funds from CoinGecko's portfolio tags; this is their one place on the page), **raised** (the rounds, amounts and lead investors DefiLlama knows) and **links**. Then **Right now**, a row of cells each shown only when there is something to say and each a link into its section: the next unlock inside 90 days, the project's wallets' last move with the 30-day outflow, the holder shift week on week, the last pushed event with its 24h move, an open **governance** vote on Snapshot (title, days left, votes, the leading choice), **attention** (items this week against the project's own median week over eight weeks — quiet, normal or loud, with a small bar strip; a count, never a sentiment), and the dossier **verdict** when a dossier exists. Below the cells, **Peers**: a small table of the three nearest projects the desk follows by label — where a peer's label differs it says so — with float, next cliff, 30-day wallet flow, 7-day move and last item; a dash means the desk has no number for that peer yet. Then one fold per block, each with its count: **Markets** (venues by 24h volume with share and spread), **Governance** (open and recent proposals), **Event moves** (how this project's own events moved on median after 24 hours and 7 days, by kind). Then the **Timeline**: the price line on the desk's chart engine. The 7d · 30d · 90d · 1y chips choose the window the chart opens on; a year of history is loaded behind it, so scroll out or drag to see further back — hover for the crosshair and the readout (price, TVL when toggled, and the count of events that day or week), scroll to zoom, drag to pan, double-click to reset. The desk's layers sit in **lanes under the price**, not on the line: the **events** lane carries a flag per day with the kind's letter (L launch · X listing · P partnership · T tokenomics · G governance · S security · M team · F funding · R metrics · U supply · W wallets · O other), filled when the desk pushed it and hollow when it went to the digest, with a count badge when several landed on one day (markers that would overprint on the long ranges collapse into one pill with
the count; zoom in and they spread out again); the **wallets** lane carries the project's wallet transfers as diamonds — filled for outflows, hollow for inflows, sized by the day's dollars — and holder-drift days as small squares. Unlock cliffs are dashed gold lines the full height with their share of circulating supply at the top (scheduled ones in a shaded strip to the right of today), and DefiLlama's dated milestones are small markers along the top. Hover a marker for its card; click it to pin the card, which links to the feed row, the post, the Supply card or the Recent moves. Under the price, the **Events** panel (a chip beside TVL; on by default) stacks the events per day on the short ranges or per week on the long ones, one colour per kind, so the cadence of what the project says and does is a shape rather than a count; click a bar to jump to that day's or week's rows in the feed. A **TVL** toggle draws DefiLlama's total value locked as a dashed line on its own scale. ## Signals

The **Signals** tab under Following is what the desk read from its own numbers: one name, one kind, one day. An **E** read is an event the price has not answered (a material event with a flat two-day move net of the market) or a routine one it over-answered; an **X** read is a move net of the market with no news behind it, or a single day past the name's own typical move. Every read shows who, what, what it is measured against, and the horizon; click a row for the evidence, what the read expects, what would make it noise, and its grade. Grades are the name's move net of BTC and the roster at +24h and +7d, so a read is right or wrong on the name's own doing, not the market's. The scoreboard beside the stream keeps every family's hit rate; a family reaches Telegram only once it has earned it, so what you see here is research, not a push. **Today** shows the five strongest reads of the last two days, and the Events page's movers line is now the market-adjusted move with the raw move on hover.

 A **Price · Lanes** switch beside the range chips turns the chart into a swimlane view: the price goes away and every kind of event gets its own row, with rows for cliffs, wallet moves, holder drift and milestones under them, each labelled with its count. Read down a column for everything that happened that week, across a row for how often one kind happens. Zoom, hover cards, the rail and the legend work the same in both views. The legend under the chart is a set of switches: click a kind for just that kind (click it again for everything), shift-click to add or remove one, **show all** to reset; cliffs, wallet moves, holder drift and milestones switch the same way, and the Events panel and the rail follow the lens. Beside the chart, the **events rail** lists what is in view, newest first by day: each event with its kind chip, the +24h move and a link to the post, holder-drift days, every wallet transfer with its counterparty and dollars, cliffs with their share of circulating, DefiLlama milestones; zoom or pan and the list follows. Hover a row and its marker glows on the chart; hover a marker and its rows light up; click a row to jump to the feed row, the Recent moves or the Supply card. The chevron on the rail's edge collapses it (remembered); on a phone it sits under the chart. The events feed sits under the chart. The **category** and **backed by** chips in the project's profile are links. A category page gathers everything the desk knows about the group: the projects you follow in it with price, 24h, 7d, market cap, attention state and the week's events; the category's market-wide numbers from CoinGecko; the category's top coins the desk does not follow yet, each with a Follow button that adds it to the roster; and what the group said and did — eight weeks of events stacked by kind, then the feed across the members. A backer page is the same lens on an investor's portfolio. On the project page, a **vs category** tile puts the project's 7d move and events this week beside its peers' medians. **Supply** and **Wallets & holders** follow when there is data, and **Under the hood** — the sources read, the people, the last 60 items with how each was read, and when and by whom the project was added — folded at the bottom. The ⋯ menu on any roster row opens the project's site, X, docs, chart, explorer and dossier, copies the contract, and adds the token to your watchlist.

### Supply

A project page carries a **Supply** section when a documented unlock schedule exists for its token. It opens with two bars: how much of the maximum supply is released (the next cliff ticked where it will land) and what is still locked by cohort — team, investors, treasury and so on as the schedule has them, with anything the schedule does not place yet shown as one hatched "later" segment. Under them, when something is scheduled ahead, the schedule itself as a chart: cumulative supply unlocked from a month back to a year ahead, cliffs as steps, linear emission as a slope, a today line; hover for the running total and each cohort's share. Then the numbers — circulating supply, the share still locked, the next cliff (date, cohort, size against circulating supply and against a month of volume) and the linear run-rate — and the cliffs table, folded past eight rows. The schedule comes from DefiLlama's documented unlocks or, for Solana tokens it does not carry, the token's Streamflow escrows. Cliffs land in the Events feed as `unlock cliff` items a week ahead, the day before and on the day; only the week-ahead sighting of a large cliff is pushed. Curated calendars lag OTC deals and postponed tranches, so the card links the schedule and says its source.

### Wallets

A project page carries a **Wallets & holders** section once the desk knows whose some addresses are. It opens with **Flows** — the dollars the project's wallets sent each week above the line and received below it, stacked by counterparty class (exchanges, DEX, market makers, bridges, other own wallets, labelled other, unlabelled) over 90 days — then the **Recent moves** table (ten rows, "all" for the rest), where a move the desk tied to an announcement carries an *announced* chip that opens it. **Holders** shows one bar of who holds the supply today — the project's own wallets, exchanges, market makers, locked, staked, pools, burned, other labelled holders, unlabelled wallets inside the top holders, and the rest of supply — and, once a few days of snapshots exist, a line of top-10 free float, exchange share and own share over time; the daily table sits behind a toggle. The **Registry** folds at the bottom under its counts; open it to see whose each address is: the treasury, the foundation, vesting and locker contracts, governance timelocks, the token contract, multisigs with their signer count (Safe 9/12) and, folded below, the signers themselves. Every row says its source and tier — the project's own docs, a Safe or Squads multisig read on-chain, the dossier's holder classes, or an open label set — so you know how much to trust it. The same registry names holders in a dossier: a top wallet that is the project's treasury is called that in the distribution findings instead of showing as an anonymous whale. It is a registry, not a ledger: it says whose a wallet is, never what a transfer means.

Below the registry, **Recent moves** is the ledger: the desk watches those wallets move the project's token, every 15 minutes, on EVM chains and Solana. Each move says who sent what to whom — an exchange, a DEX pool, a market maker, another of the project's own wallets, or an unlabelled wallet — with its size against circulating supply. Large outflows land in the Events feed and, with the **Wallets** toggle on, as a push; inflows and internal moves stay in the digest. The wording is deliberately flat: a transfer to an exchange is reported as a transfer to an exchange, never as a sale. The card also shows what share of the last month's outflow went to counterparties nobody has labelled yet.

When an outflow follows an announcement — a treasury proposal that passed, a published supply change, a raise, a listing — the item cites it ("Announced 2026-10-18: …"). That is a citation, not a verdict: the desk shows you the two facts side by side and leaves the reading to you.

**Holders**, under the ledger, is the daily picture of who holds the top of the book: the top-10 free float, the project's own wallets, exchanges, market makers, locked and staked supply, pools. A sharp week-on-week shift lands as a `holder drift` event using the same bars as the watchlist.

A dossier on a token the desk follows carries a short **following** strip under its links — still locked, next cliff, wallets watched, the last move — pointing at the project page.

## Watchlist

The tokens you watch. A watch re-runs the cheap chain legs (safety, holders, liquidity, RugCheck) hourly for a day, then every six hours, and alerts only when something changed: a worsened finding, a liquidity drop, a lock drained, the top 10 rising, the dev selling. The table shows when you started, how many checks ran, the last and next check, and the last change. Click a row for the dossier; × stops watching.

**★ Watch** on any dossier adds a token here, and so does the ⋯ menu on a project row. The mechanics are in [Watch](/dossier/watch).

## Where it goes

Pushed events reach the Telegram channel with the `following` tag. The project page links to the token's dossier when it has a contract, and a dossier of a followed project links back here. The narratives around these projects live under [Radar › Narratives](/signals/narratives).
