---
title: "What it can't tell you"
description: "The honest edges: what a dossier does not read, where a model is involved, and what a verdict is not."
---

- **It is not investment advice.** A dossier is an automated read of public on-chain and social data at the time shown. Nothing on the site is a recommendation to buy or sell.
- **PASS is not "scam".** It means a critical check failed or could not be read, or the picture is too weak. Many honest tokens PASS because their source is unverified or their liquidity is thin.
- **SMALL is not "buy".** It says nothing vetoed and the readable picture is clean. It is a size word.
- **Unknown stays unknown.** A provider that was down, a chain a simulator does not cover, a bio that says nothing — the dossier says so instead of filling the gap. Look for the PARTIAL banner and the `??` count.
- **Team membership needs a claim.** If a founder never says so in their own bio or posts, and no repo or third party names them, the team line stays empty. That is a limit, not a bug: the alternative is listing everyone the project ever retweeted.
- **Models can be wrong** in the description, the read of X and the story. They are asked to cite; check the citation before you rely on the line.
- **Private data is never read.** Follows are public follows; profile fields are public fields. The site has no wallet access and never asks for a key.
- **Chains not in the configuration** are not supported; the run says so.
- **Telegram and Discord intake** are not live yet; X, FOMO, YouTube and the Robinhood-chain radar are.

If a line on a dossier is wrong, use **Report a finding** on the card. Fixtures are added for every confirmed miss so it cannot come back.
