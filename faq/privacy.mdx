## About you

Your account holds your display name, email, plan, the X handle you chose and its public follows, your search history, watchlist, notes, asked questions and usage rows. All of it is per-user: another account never sees your history, your stars or your notes' authorship. You can export history and watchlist as CSV and delete the account from the account page; deletion removes every row that is yours.

Dossiers you ran stay in the shared library, without your name. They are reads of public on-chain data.

## About tokens and accounts on dossiers

On-chain facts — addresses, holders, transactions, contracts — are public records. Profile fields of X accounts shown on a dossier (bio, follower count, age, self-declared location) are public fields, read when you ask.

A third party who appears on a dossier and wants a profile field removed can ask through the **Privacy** page's removal form; the request lands with the desk and is handled by hand.

## Sharing

A permalink `/dd/<chain>/<address>` shows the cached dossier to anyone, with no per-viewer data — no stars, no notes' authors, no history. Signed-in users opening a permalink are taken to the live page.

The full terms and privacy notice are at `/terms` and `/privacy` on the site.
