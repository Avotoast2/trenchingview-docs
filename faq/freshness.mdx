---
title: "How fresh is what I see?"
description: "Chain data is live every time; social and narrative legs are reused inside fixed windows; Fresh bypasses all of it."
---

- **Chain data is live** in every mode — contract, deployer, holders, flow, liquidity are read at run time.
- **Social legs** (the X sweep, team, X history, prior coins, the Grok read) are reused for **6 hours**; the **description, claim type and substance** pass for **24 hours**; the **Story** for 24 hours. A finding that came from the cache carries a *cached from* mark in its data.
- **Fresh** bypasses every cache.
- **Watch** re-checks the chain legs hourly for the first day, then every six hours.
- **Videos** channels are polled every 30 minutes; view counts are re-read at +1h, +6h, +24h, +3d, +7d.
- **Outcome snapshots** land at +2m, +1h, +12h and +7d after the desk first sees a token.
- **Notable followers**: live checks are reused for 24 hours; the followings index refreshes small accounts weekly and large ones monthly.
- The **registry** of official stock-token wrappers refreshes every 6 hours.

Every dossier states the time of its read under the banner. A permalink shows the cached dossier with that timestamp; re-run for a current one.
