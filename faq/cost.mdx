---
title: "What does a dossier cost?"
description: "One credit for a full run, nothing for quick runs, nothing when the cache did the work."
---

**A credit is one full dossier.** The chain reads are free; the credit pays for the X and Grok legs.

- **Quick** is always free, within your plan's hourly limit.
- **Full** costs one credit — unless both expensive legs came from the shared cache (the last 6 hours for social, 24 for description) or the run cost less than a cent, in which case it costs nothing.
- **Fresh** and **Story** always cost one.
- **Watch** re-checks, **Find** searches and the **Videos** section are free. AskAura **Answer** is free of credits and counted against a daily allowance.

Charges are settled when a run completes, from what actually ran. A stopped run is not charged. The ledger on your [account page](/getting-started/account) shows every charge with its reason.

The **checks** tile on each card shows what the run cost the desk in provider calls. That is informational; your credit is the only thing charged to you.

Plan allowances and prices are on [Plans and credits](/getting-started/credits).
