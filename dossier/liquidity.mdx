The Liquidity section is the map of where the token can actually be bought and sold. It reads the raw pair list the resolver already fetched — no extra network — and shows every pool above a dust floor.

## The read line

One line with the state and the numbers behind it: how many pools, total liquidity across them, and the state word:

| State | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **concentrated** | One pool holds most of the liquidity — the normal healthy shape |
| **fractured** | The top pool holds less than 60% of the total — exits are split across venues |
| **thin** | Total liquidity across every pool under $5,000 — an exit trap, RED |

A price gap above 2% between deep pools (each above $5,000) is flagged YELLOW: the venues disagree on what the token is worth.

## The pools table

| Column | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **pool** | DEX and pool type (Uniswap v4, Raydium CPMM, Meteora DLMM…) |
| **pair** | Base / quote |
| **liquidity** | USD in the pool |
| **share** | The pool's share of total liquidity, as a bar |
| **vol 24h** / **txns 24h** | Activity |
| **price** | The pool's own price |
| **age** | Pool age |

Pools under $1,000 are counted as dust — listed, not judged. DexScreener caps the per-token list at 30 pairs; a capped map says so with `+`.

## Quote mix

What the token is paired against, in shares of liquidity: **stable**, **native** (ETH, SOL, BNB), **stock token** (an official tokenized equity on Robinhood chain), or another token. The quote kind also appears as a chip in the header: a pool quoted in a stock token means the token's price moves with the equity.
