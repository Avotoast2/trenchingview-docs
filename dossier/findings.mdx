The Findings grid is the evidence under the verdict. Every check returns a **status** and an **evidence** line — a sentence with the numbers and addresses it read, never an adjective. Clean checks fold away when there is something worse to see; open them to read what passed.

## Statuses

| Status | Meaning | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| **RED** | The check failed | On a critical check: veto → PASS. Otherwise it costs score |
| **YELLOW** | Worth knowing; not a failure | Costs score |
| **OK** | Passed | |
| **UNKNOWN** | Could not be read | On a critical check: treated as RED → veto |
| **INFO** | A fact with no judgement | Unscored |

Critical checks are the ones a rug turns on: sellability, taxes, transfer fees, verified source, RugCheck's risks, an explorer scam flag, a serial launcher, a pulled pool. See [Fail-closed](/concepts/fail-closed).

## Pillars

Six pillars carry the score (weights out of 100). Social and Project findings render in their own sections rather than the grid.

| Pillar | Weight | Covers |
|---|---|---|
| **safety** | 25 | Honeypot / sellability, taxes, mint · pause · blacklist · proxy · hidden owner, verified source, LP lock, explorer flags, bytecode signatures, Solana freeze / mint / transfer-fee / mutable-metadata authorities |
| **deployer** | 20 | Wallet freshness, funding trail (bridges and solvers), serial launches, dev holding, launch-tx whitelists, pre-computed exemptions, one operator funding many launch buyers |
| **distribution** | 20 | Top-10 and single-wallet share on the **free float** (bonds, staking pools and LPs classified out), smart-account and bundler wallets unwrapped, fresh-wallet holders, holder clusters funded by one wallet, liquidity floor, the liquidity map |
| **flow** | 15 | Same-block bundles, snipers in the first seconds, rotation seeding (sell → equal rebuy), wash volume, fee extraction to bridges and exchanges, insider outflow to exchanges |
| **social** | 10 | Official account, dev account, trusted voices, promo farms, the Grok read, identity check, recycled accounts, prior coins, team discovery, community takeover, copycats |
| **project** | 10 | Website, domain age, repo shape, AI-generated code and copy, site builder, claim type, substance |

## Data quality

When a public API was rate-limiting during the run, a **data quality** line lists the degraded hosts. Unknowns above it may be transient; re-run later.
