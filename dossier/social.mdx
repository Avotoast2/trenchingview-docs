The Social section is the people layer. It reads the official account, sweeps X for who is talking and how, and separates three groups that raw feeds blur together: the team, the accounts you already trust, and everyone else.

## The read line

One sentence from the Grok read — what X actually says about the project — followed by the identity line: whether X agrees that the linked official account is who runs the project. A **copycat** (the linked account is not who X thinks runs it) is called out here.

## Tiles

| Tile | What it measures |
|---|---|
| **accounts** / **posts** | How many distinct accounts and posts the sweep found |
| **bots** | Grok's estimate of the bot share, as *est.* |
| **farm-shaped** / **templated** | Tiny fresh accounts posting the same template minutes apart |
| **sentiment** | Bull / bear split, *est.* |
| **engagements** | Likes and replies on what was found |
| **trusted voices** | How many accounts you follow are talking |
| **notable followers** | How many prominent accounts follow the official, team or dev |

Tiles are coloured by status only; a number without a judgement stays grey.

## Trusted voices

Posts about the token from accounts you follow, newest first, each linking to the post. If one of them is warning, it is the first thing here. Your follows come from the handle you set at onboarding; they are read once and when you refresh them.

## Notable followers

Which prominent accounts — venture firms and partners, founders and executives, technologists, research and press — follow the official account, the founder or the dev. A curated roster, never traders; tier-one accounts are checked live and cached a day, the rest come from a followings index that also carries **since at least** dates and unfollows. A notable that follows the founder is one of the strongest signals the site has, which is why a dossier reads the followers of a silent official account to find who the founder is.

## Around it

Accounts the official quotes, reposts or follows that **claim no role** on the project. They are shown in one muted line so the signal is not lost, and they are kept off the team line. See [Who is behind it](/concepts/who-is-behind-it).

## Theses and videos

- **FOMO theses** — calls on this token by traders on fomo.family, ★ when you follow them, click to open.
- **Videos** — what was said about this token on the YouTube channels the desk watches, with the timestamp; click the time to watch from there. See [Videos](/signals/videos).

## Flags and sources

Red and green flags from the read of X, each backed by the post URL it came from. A flag without a citation is dropped. The sources row lists the citations behind the read, numbered.
