---

**Story** is the one section that is allowed to be narrative. It is built from a search of X with citations, and from the facts the dossier already established, and it is kept separate from the description on purpose: a description never carries chatter or price talk, and a story never restates launch facts as if it had verified them.

## What it contains

- **The chatter read** — one line: what people are saying, in aggregate.
- **Facts that are narrative** — first call, why now, the peak so far, prior history of the team or the ticker, the origin of the name, the platform and the launch. Each fact carries its footnotes.
- **Two columns of prose** with superscript citations, and a **Sources** row of the posts behind them.
- **Model and cost** in the header, and a **refresh** control.

## When it runs

Story runs after a dossier finishes, from the Full and Fresh modes, and costs **one credit** (it is the lore leg). It is reused for 24 hours. A Quick dossier does not run it.

<Note>
The story is what X says, cited. It is not what the desk verified. Anything in it that matters to a decision should also appear in Findings or Substance — and if it does not, that is the point.
</Note>
