The last section on the page is the token's own history on the desk.

## Earlier dossiers

Every run on this token, newest first: when, the verdict chip, the score, what killed it, and where the run came from (web, radar, intake, watch, a test). More than six fold behind **show all N runs**. A token that was PASS three times and WATCH once tells you something the single latest verdict does not.

## Outcomes

When a token is first seen by the desk — from any source — it is snapshotted at four horizons:

| Horizon | Read |
|---|---|
| **+2m** | Price, market cap, liquidity two minutes after first sight |
| **+1h** | One hour after |
| **+12h** | Twelve hours after |
| **+7d** | Seven days after |

A token whose pool is gone reads **dead** in the price column. These snapshots are what the desk learns from: they are the outcome side of every call and every verdict, and the raw material for the studies that calibrate the checks.

<Note>
Outcomes are recorded from the first sight, not from your run. If the desk saw the token days before you did, the horizons are already filled in when you open it.
</Note>
