Everything the desk finds lands on the site (Today, the Events feed, your Watchlist). **Alerts** is the push leg: a Telegram chat you link, and the kinds of alert you want in it.

## Link Telegram

Press **Link Telegram**. The bot opens with a one-time code; press **Start** and the chat is bound to your account. For a group, add the bot to the group and send the `/start <code>` line there. A code lives 24 hours and works once; the page notices the link within a few seconds. Several chats can be linked; × unlinks one, and `/alerts off` in the chat does the same from the Telegram side.

## What you receive

| Toggle | Fires when |
|---|---|
| **Following** | The table pushes an event from a project you follow — a launch, a listing, a partnership, a token-economics change |
| **Watchlist** | A re-check finds a change on a token you watch — a worsened finding, a liquidity drop, a lock drained, the top 10 rising, the dev selling |
| **Narratives** | A narrative turns confirmed or emerging |
| **Videos** | A video with a thesis or a trade was processed |
| **Unlocks** | A cliff unlock a week out on a project you follow, when it is at least 1% of circulating supply or a quarter of a month's volume |
| **Wallets** | A project's own wallet (treasury, foundation, vesting) moved a large amount of its token to an exchange, a DEX pool, a market maker or an unlabelled wallet |

All six are on by default. Watchlist alerts are personal: only the chats linked to the account that watches the token receive them.

## Quiet hours

A window in your local time. Alerts inside it are dropped, not queued; the site still shows them. Clear both fields to switch quiet hours off.

## Test

**Send a test alert** pushes one message to every chat linked to your account, ignoring quiet hours, so you can confirm the link.
