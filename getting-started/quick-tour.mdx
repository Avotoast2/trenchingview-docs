## 0. Sign in

TrenchingView is invite-only while in active development. Redeem your invite code, tell it which X account you are (optional — that is your ★ trust layer), and you land on **Due diligence**. Details in [Account](/getting-started/account).

## 1. Paste

Paste a contract address (EVM `0x…` or a Solana mint), a ticker, or a whole forwarded message — the address is picked out. To force a chain for a ticker, add it after the name: `BONK sol`. If a ticker matches several tokens, you get a **Which one?** list ranked by liquidity; nothing is picked for you.

## 2. Pick a mode

| Mode | Reads | Time | Cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Quick** | chain only: contract, deployer, holders, launch flow, liquidity | 15–30 s | free |
| **Full** (default) | everything, with recent social and description legs reused from the shared cache | 30–120 s | 1 credit (0 when served from cache) |
| **Fresh** | everything, cache bypassed | 30–120 s | 1 credit |

`Enter` runs. `Esc` stops a run in flight — the stage in progress finishes, nothing after it runs, nothing is saved.

## 3. Read the card

The verdict banner comes first: **PASS** (vetoed — the banner names what killed it), **WATCH** or **SMALL**. Under it, the header: symbol and quote asset, chain, launchpad, DEX, the address with a copy button, and the tiles (price, FDV, liquidity, 24h volume, pair age, checks and cost).

<Tip>
A PASS on a token you like is the most useful thing the site produces. The banner tells you which check vetoed; the [Findings](/dossier/findings) section shows the evidence behind it.
</Tip>

## 4. Walk the sections

The rail under the header jumps to each section:

- **Project** — what it is and who is behind it. The team line lists only accounts that claim the project in their own words. See [Project](/dossier/project).
- **Substance** — what exists, which claims could be checked, the dated footprint, and what was not found.
- **Social** — the official account, the read of X, trusted voices, notable followers, promoters.
- **Story** — the crowd narrative with citations, kept apart from the facts.
- **Findings** — every check, worst first, by pillar.
- **Liquidity** — where it trades and how fractured that is.
- **Before** — earlier dossiers on the same token and how the price behaved after first sight.

## 5. Act on it

- **★ Watch** re-checks the cheap chain legs hourly for a day, then every six hours, and alerts you only when something changed. See [Watch](/dossier/watch).
- **Add a note** — one line, searchable in the corpus, shown on the card for everyone.
- **Share** — the permalink `/dd/<chain>/<address>` shows the cached dossier to anyone, no account needed.
- **Report a finding** — tell us when a line is wrong; it lands with the desk.

## 6. Ask

**AskAura** (the ✦ button, or the nav tab) sits on top of everything the desk has seen: dossiers, stories, theses, calls, notes, videos. "Which Robinhood-chain tokens had a bundle this week?" is one query. See [AskAura](/signals/askaura).
