TrenchingView is a **conviction machine, not a sniping machine**. You paste a contract address or a ticker; about a minute later you have a dossier that has read the contract, the deployer, the holders, the launch window, the liquidity, the project's own site and code, and what X actually says about it — and a verdict that fails closed.

The north-star metric is **time to no**. Most tokens in the trenches deserve a fast, well-argued no. The dossier is built to give you that quickly, and to make the rare yes something you can defend line by line.

## What a dossier is

<Steps>
  <Step title="Resolve — the address is the identity">
    A contract address is the token. A ticker is only a nickname: when a ticker is ambiguous, TrenchingView shows the candidates and lets you pick. It never guesses silently.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check — dozens of independent reads">
    Contract safety (honeypot, taxes, mint and freeze authorities, unverified source), deployer forensics (freshness, funding trail, serial launches, whitelists), holder concentration on the **free float** (bonds, pools and LPs classified out), launch-window flow (bundles, snipers, rotation), the liquidity map, the project itself (site, domain, repo, description, substance), and the social layer (official account, team, trusted voices, notable followers, a cited read of X).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verdict — fail-closed">
    A critical check that fails **or cannot be read** vetoes the token. Unknown is never treated as fine. Only with zero vetoes does the score decide between **WATCH** and **SMALL**. See [Fail-closed](/concepts/fail-closed).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Source — facts, not adjectives">
    Every line carries where it came from. Models extract and describe; they never grade. Numbers appear only when they were measured. Where a provider was down, the dossier says **PARTIAL** rather than filling the gap.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Your trust layer">
    The X accounts you follow are marked ★ on every dossier — among trusted voices, team and promoters. That layer is computed from *your* follows, never from ours.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What it is not

- **Not investment advice.** A dossier is an automated read of public on-chain and social data at the time shown. Re-run before acting.
- **Not a sniper.** Nothing here is optimised to be first. It is optimised to be right about the things that end a trade.
- **Not a model's opinion.** Where a model is involved (the project description, the read of X, the story), it is asked to extract and cite. Judgement stays with you.
