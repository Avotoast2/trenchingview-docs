title: "Today"
description: "The home page once you are signed in: what changed since you last looked, newest first, with every block opening its section."
---

**Today** is where you land. It reads the rest of the site for you and shows what moved since your last visit; each block is a shortcut into its section.

| Block | What it shows | Opens |
|---|---|---|
| **KPI strip** | Pushed today · digest · live and emerging narratives · projects followed | — |
| **Pushed** | Events the table pushed from the projects you follow, newest first, with the move since the event; the movers strip ranks the last two days by reaction. "New since you last looked" counts against the last time you opened Today or the Events feed. | [Following › Events](/signals/following#events) |
| **Your watchlist** | The tokens you watch whose re-check found a change in the last two days; otherwise the list with its last checks | [Following › Watchlist](/signals/following#watchlist) |
| **Radar** | Narratives new this week (or the top live ones) and the stock tokens that are igniting, hot or cooling with their implied open and premium | [Radar › Narratives](/signals/narratives), [Stock tokens](/signals/stock-token-radar) |
| **Research** | Videos processed in the last two days with their theses and trades | [Research › Videos](/signals/videos) |
| **Your recent dossiers** | The tokens you checked, newest first, with the verdict and score | [Dossier](/dossier/running) |

The page refreshes the pushed and watchlist blocks every minute while it is open. An empty block says why it is empty ("nothing pushed in the last two days", "nothing watched yet") rather than hiding.

Telegram is the push leg of the same information: choose what reaches your phone on [Alerts](/getting-started/alerts).
