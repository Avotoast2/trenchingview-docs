## The unit: one credit = one full dossier

The expensive parts of a dossier are the social legs — the read of X and the Grok read — and the story. Everything on-chain is free to read. So the meter is simple:

| Run | Credits |
|---|---|
| **Quick** dossier (chain only) | 0, always — within the hourly limit |
| **Full** dossier | 1 — but **0** when both expensive legs were served from the shared cache, or the run cost less than a cent |
| **Fresh** dossier (cache bypassed) | 1 |
| **Story** (the lore leg) | 1 |
| TrenchBrain **Find** | 0 |
| TrenchBrain **Answer** | 0 credits; counted against your daily answers |
| Watch re-checks | 0 (chain legs only) |

Charges happen at completion, from what actually ran. A run that was stopped is not charged.

## Caps that protect you and the desk

Two limits sit behind the plan numbers:

- **A daily spend cap per plan.** If the day's provider spend has hit your cap, a Full request is downgraded to Quick and the card says so, instead of failing.
- **A global daily ceiling** on the desk's own provider spend. Past it, everyone's Full requests run Quick until midnight UTC. Rare, and always announced on the run.

When a submit is refused (concurrency, hourly quick limit, credits at zero) the page says exactly which limit and shows the meter. Nothing is charged before a run.

## Reading the meter

The strip under the search box on the Due diligence page shows credits left, today's spend, runs in flight and quick runs this hour. The full ledger — every charge with its reason and the dossier it paid for — is on your [account page](/getting-started/account).
